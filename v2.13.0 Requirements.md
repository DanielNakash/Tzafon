## 1. Scope of v2.13.0

v2.13.0 is a **redesign increment**: it rebuilds two surfaces the user touches on every task, and changes the rules behind them where the old rules were broken or unexplainable.

1. **The task editor becomes three panes — Task · Timing · Alignment** (deck slides 1–6). The single scrolling column with eight modal sheets is replaced by three tabbed panes edited in place. The edit scope of a repeating task is asked **once, at Save**, over a list of what changed, instead of by a banner chosen before editing. Both dates are set on **one shared calendar**; repeat settings sit inline in a **repeat card**; and a repeating task's per-occurrence deadline becomes **an offset of N days after each to-do date** (Room 5→6).
2. **Holding a list-row checkbox opens a new status sheet** (deck slides 7–11). A tap still marks a task done (or open), now with an **Undo toast**; a 350 ms hold raises a sheet that shows all five statuses and what each one does. For a repeating task, freezing, thawing, closing and reopening now act on **the whole series** when asked to, which they never could before.

Around those two surfaces the version also settles **one vocabulary app-wide** (Status, Backlog, Notes, To-do date, Deadline, Repeat, ticks / moves / serves), and makes **every calendar follow the week-start setting**.

## 2. Data model deltas (Room)

**Room 5 → 6, additive** (`NFR-DATA-1`, `NFR-DATA-1.1`). One column and one enum value, for the per-occurrence deadline offset (`FR-REC-4.1`, ADR 0001):

| Change | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `series.dueOffsetDays INTEGER` (nullable) | `SeriesEntity`, domain `Series`, `SeriesSpec`, `RecurrenceDraft` | 0–30 (`MAX_DUE_OFFSET_DAYS`). Null on every series that isn't `DueMode.OFFSET`. |
| `DueMode.OFFSET` | `domain/recurrence` | Stored by name in the existing `dueMode` column; no column change. |

- **The migration** is one `ALTER TABLE series ADD COLUMN dueOffsetDays INTEGER`. Migrations are now one list, `TzafonDatabase.MIGRATIONS`. Never a destructive fallback.
- **`DueMode.RECURRING` and `dueRule` stay on purpose** — series saved before v2.13.0 still carry them and the engine still computes them (`FR-REC-4.3`, ADR 0001). They are not dead code.
- **Schemas are now exported** to `android/app/schemas/` (`exportSchema = true`). `5.json` was generated from the committed v5 entity, `6.json` is current. Never regenerate an older version's JSON from newer entities.
- **No other schema change.** Series-scoped freeze, thaw, close and reopen use the existing `Series.frozen`, `Series.endDate` and `Series.exceptions` (`FR-REC-5.11`). Repeat off and forward delete use `endDate` (`FR-REC-6.12`, `FR-REC-6.14`).

**Export document: format version stays `2`** (`DM-EXPORT-1`). The export and the Firestore codec are Gson-reflective over the domain shapes, so `dueOffsetDays` and `OFFSET` (serialized by name, `DM-EXPORT-1.5`) travel with no code change. A **v2.12.0 export without the field still imports** (the field falls to null, `DM-EXPORT-1.8`), and a **pre-v2.13.0 Firestore document reads with no offset**. Importing a v2.13.0 export into an older app is not a supported path.

**Transient, never stored:**

- `TaskDraft.habitAmount` holds the "How much?" answer until Save (`FR-EDITOR-3.4`). It is not a task field.
- The editor's `EditorSession` (opening snapshot, draft, active tab) is kept in the saved-state bundle through `EditorSessionCodec` (`FR-EDITOR-1.9`), not in DataStore or Room.
- The undo toast's snapshot (`StatusSnapshot`: the task plus its task-sourced habit logs and goal contributions) lives in memory for the toast's lifetime only (`FR-STATUS-4.2`).

**Carried untouched:** `Task.themeId`. The editor no longer shows or edits it, and its only writer is deleted (`FR-EDITOR-6.8`), but existing values keep riding through Room, Firestore and the export.

**Per-device preferences: no new key.** The existing `week_start` setting is now also read by every calendar grid (`FR-SET-1.1`).

## 3. Functional requirements

### 3.1 Series-scoped freeze, thaw, close and reopen (`FR-REC-5`, `DM-TASK-2` — refined)

*Source: [Closing or freezing one occurrence vs the whole series](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/2) (deck slides 8, 10, 11). Built in M1 (`StateMachine`, `RoomTaskRepository.setState`); the sheet that asks is §3.12. Refines v2.0.0's `FR-REC-5` ("freeze terminates a series after a confirmation prompt; thaw restores it") and `DM-TASK-2` (thaw always restores recurrence).*

Before v2.13.0 a repeating task could only be frozen as a whole (behind a separate confirmation dialog), and closed only one occurrence at a time. There was no way to end a series from its status, and thawing always restored it.

`FR-REC-5` **A status change on a repeating task reaches either this occurrence or the whole series. Freezing always takes the whole series; thawing, closing and reopening a closed series ask which; Open and Done otherwise reach only the occurrence.**

- **`FR-REC-5.1` No freezing one occurrence.** Frozen on a repeating task never offers "this one". The status sheet shows one explanation and a **Freeze series** button, which is the confirmation; the `ConfirmFreezeSeries` dialog is gone.
- **`FR-REC-5.2` Freezing the series** marks it frozen. The chosen occurrence becomes Frozen and is kept; everything dated before it is untouched; **every later occurrence still Open is deleted, edited or not**; later Done and Closed occurrences are kept.
- **`FR-REC-5.3` Thawing asks** while the series is frozen (`StateMachine.askFor` → `Ask.Scope`), even when the target is Open or Done:
  - **Just this one:** only the occurrence changes; the series stays frozen.
  - **The whole series, to Open or Done:** the series thaws; the occurrence takes the chosen status; occurrences from today on come back from the rule. Edits made to the occurrences deleted by the freeze are lost.
  - **The whole series, to Closed:** the series closes (`FR-REC-5.6`).
- **`FR-REC-5.4` A leftover Frozen occurrence of a live series** (the series was thawed from another occurrence) thaws alone and never asks.
- **`FR-REC-5.5` Closed on a repeating task asks.** "Just this one" closes the occurrence, as before.
- **`FR-REC-5.6` Closing the whole series:**
  1. the series' **end date becomes today**, whatever the chosen occurrence's date;
  2. every occurrence **dated today or earlier that isn't Done becomes Closed** — overdue, edited and Frozen ones included;
  3. every occurrence **dated after today that isn't Done is deleted**, so its date can come back on reopen;
  4. Done occurrences are never touched;
  5. on a frozen series, closing **clears the frozen mark** — a series is never both frozen and closed.
- **`FR-REC-5.7` Reopening a closed series.** A series is *ended* when its end date is today or earlier (`SeriesStatus.of(series, today)`). Two ways back, both allowed:
  - in the editor, set Ends to Never or a later date for the series (`FR-EDITOR-5.7`);
  - in the status sheet, move a Closed occurrence of an ended series to Open or Done with **The whole series**. The end date is cleared to Never (the date before the close isn't remembered — that would need a new field).

  Occurrences from today on come back. **Past occurrences are never changed**; those the close set to Closed stay Closed. A Closed occurrence of a live series (a future end date) moves alone and never asks.
- **`FR-REC-5.8` The past stays gone.** The days a series spent frozen or closed are recorded as series exceptions, so neither the thaw / reopen nor any later top-up backfills them as overdue rows (the old thaw did, because generation enumerates from the anchor).
- **`FR-REC-5.9` A whole-series choice always acts,** even when the occurrence is already in the target status: Frozen on a Frozen occurrence still freezes the series, and Closed with the whole series on a Closed occurrence still closes it.
- **`FR-REC-5.10` "Earlier" and "later" use the occurrence's to-do date,** falling back to its slot date — the date the user sees on an edited occurrence.
- **`FR-REC-5.11` No schema change.** `Series.frozen`, `Series.endDate` and `Series.exceptions` carry it all (§2).
- **`FR-REC-5.12` No `DM-NOT` surface.** Ending or pausing a series is not a miss; nothing counts it.

`DM-TASK-2` **(refined) Thaw** (Frozen → …): the user chooses the target status **and, while the series is frozen, the scope** (`FR-REC-5.3`). → Open keeps the to-do date, and restores the series **only with The whole series**. → Done applies all Done actions (unchanged), and thaws the series only with The whole series. → Closed marks this occurrence Closed, or closes the whole series.

> **[DECISION] — "Ended" is "end date today or earlier"** (M1). The data can't tell a series closed from the sheet from one whose end date simply passed, and in both cases The whole series reopens it. A series with a future end date is still live, so it doesn't ask.

> **[OPEN] — Freezing a series that is already closed.** Not specified on the map. As built, it sets the frozen mark and keeps the end date, so the series is both frozen and ended — which `FR-REC-5.6`'s "never both" rule forbids from the other direction. To settle in verification.

**Acceptance criteria**

1. Freezing a repeating task asks no scope and freezes the series; the occurrence becomes Frozen; later Open occurrences (edited too) are deleted; earlier ones and later Done / Closed ones are kept (`FR-REC-5.1`, `FR-REC-5.2`). *`StateMachineTest`, `SeriesScopedStatusTest`*
2. Freezing an occurrence that is already Frozen still freezes the series (`FR-REC-5.9`). *`StateMachineTest`, `SeriesScopedStatusTest`*
3. Thawing an occurrence of a frozen series asks; Just this one leaves the series frozen; The whole series to Open fills it in from today and the frozen days never come back; to Done also counts the occurrence; to Closed closes the series (`FR-REC-5.3`, `FR-REC-5.8`, `DM-TASK-2`). *`StateMachineTest`, `SeriesScopedStatusTest`*
4. A leftover Frozen occurrence of a live series thaws alone without asking (`FR-REC-5.4`). *`StateMachineTest`, `SeriesScopedStatusTest`*
5. Closing the whole series — from any occurrence, including a later one or one already Closed — ends it today, closes what isn't Done up to today, deletes what's later, leaves Done alone and clears a frozen mark (`FR-REC-5.6`, `FR-REC-5.9`). *`SeriesScopedStatusTest`*
6. Close this one settles only the occurrence (`FR-REC-5.5`). *`StateMachineTest`, `SeriesScopedStatusTest`*
7. A Closed occurrence of an ended series asks before Open / Done; The whole series clears the end date and resumes from today with the past untouched; Just this one leaves the series closed; a Closed occurrence of a live series moves alone (`FR-REC-5.7`). *`StateMachineTest`, `SeriesScopedStatusTest`*
8. Open and Done on a repeating task never ask and ignore a whole-series scope; a one-off task never asks (`FR-REC-5`). *`StateMachineTest`*
9. Backlog on a repeating task stays forbidden (`FR-BACKLOG-2`). *`StateMachineTest`*

### 3.2 Per-occurrence deadline as an offset (`FR-REC-4` — refined)

*Source: [Repeat settings inline in the Timing pane](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/5) §2, ADR 0001. Built in M3. Refines the "singular vs. recurring Due-Date modes" clause of v2.0.0's `FR-REC-4`; the rest of `FR-REC-4` (the three patterns, the per-occurrence operations as refined by `FR-REC-6`, and "the next occurrence is computed independently of prior ones") carries.*

`FR-REC-4` **(refined) A repeating task's deadline is one of: none; One fixed (`SINGULAR` — one date shared by every occurrence, which also ends the series); or Per occurrence (`OFFSET` — each occurrence's deadline falls N days after its own to-do date, N from 0 to 30). A per-occurrence deadline can no longer be set as a second rule.**

- **`FR-REC-4.1` The offset.** `DueMode.OFFSET` with `dueOffsetDays` ∈ 0..30. The engine (`Recurrence.computeDue`, `nearestOccurrence`) gives each occurrence `toDo + dueOffsetDays`.
- **`FR-REC-4.2` Only a fixed deadline ends a series.** An offset never does.
- **`FR-REC-4.3` Old deadline rules are read-only.** A series saved with `DueMode.RECURRING` + `dueRule` keeps working in the engine, unchanged, and the oracle-parity suites stay green. The editor shows it as **"Custom deadline rule"** whose only action is **Switch to offset** (`FR-EDITOR-5.8`). No new series can be given a deadline rule.
- **`FR-REC-4.4` Switch to offset** (`Recurrence.switchToOffsetDays(spec, today)`) sets the offset to the gap between the next occurrence's to-do date and its deadline, **raised to 0** if negative and **capped at 30**; it is **0 when there is no next deadline** (the series ended, or the rule ran out). Saving it writes `dueMode = OFFSET, dueOffsetDays = n` and drops `dueRule`.
- **`FR-REC-4.5` Re-saving keeps the offset.** The editor hydrates `dueOffsetDays` and both repository save paths keep it, so re-saving an offset series (e.g. one synced from another device) never drops it.

> **[DECISION] — Cap at 30** (M3). A monthly deadline rule over a weekly to-do rule can yield a gap of ~50 days, outside the offset's range; the switch caps rather than refusing.

**Acceptance criteria**

1. An offset series gives every occurrence `toDo + N`; a new repeating task saved with an offset gets per-occurrence deadlines; editing a whole offset series to a new offset re-dates its deadlines (`FR-REC-4.1`). *`SeriesDueOffsetTest`, `RecurrenceTest`*
2. An offset series never ends at a deadline; a fixed one does (`FR-REC-4.2`). *`RecurrenceTest`*
3. A `RECURRING` series still computes its deadlines exactly as before; the oracle-parity cases are unchanged (`FR-REC-4.3`). *`RecurrenceTest`*
4. Switch to offset yields the gap, 0 for a negative gap, 30 for a larger one, and 0 with no next deadline (`FR-REC-4.4`). *`RecurrenceTest`*
5. The offset round-trips through its Room row, the Firestore codec and the export; a v2.12 export and a pre-v2.13 Firestore doc read with no offset (§2). *`SeriesDueOffsetTest`, `DataTransferTest`*
6. A v5 database holding an old deadline-rule series migrates to v6 through the app's own migrations, keeps the rule, and gains an empty offset (`NFR-DATA-1.1`). *`MigrationTest`*
7. Re-saving an offset series in the editor keeps it (`FR-REC-4.5`). *`EditorSessionTest`, `DraftDiffTest`*

### 3.3 One vocabulary, app-wide (`FR-DESIGN-6`)

*Source: [Wording: deck terms vs app terms, app-wide](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/3) (inventory of every string in `ui/**` and `notify/**` against all 11 slides). Built for the surfaces the redesign doesn't rebuild in M2; the redesigned surfaces (editor, status sheet, toast) are built in these words from the start. Refines the copy of `FR-ALL-2` / `FR-PLAN-6.2` (search placeholder), `FR-ALL-4` (horizon line), `DM-TASK-4` (cue prompt) and every "Someday" string since v2.5.0.*

`FR-DESIGN-6` **The app uses one set of words for its concepts, on every screen: the deck's words, with the owner's rulings below. The choice is app-wide, not editor-only. Code identifiers keep their names.**

- **`FR-DESIGN-6.1` Status, not state** — the editor strip ("Status"), the sheet ("Set status"), Planning's pill ("Set status"), and screen-reader copy ("…double-tap and hold to change status").
- **`FR-DESIGN-6.2` Backlog, never Someday** — including "someday / maybe" flavour text. The moon icon stays.
- **`FR-DESIGN-6.3` Notes, To-do date, Deadline.** Notes replaces Description; the dates read To-do date / Deadline, with uppercase eyebrows `TO-DO` (hyphenated) and `DEADLINE`. "Due" survives **as an adjective** (`DUE TODAY`, `DUE`, Overdue, "after it's due"); the date itself is always the deadline.
- **`FR-DESIGN-6.4` Alignment verbs.** A task **ticks** a habit and **moves** a goal; **serves** stays for links further up (goal or habit → theme, habit → goal). Habit-linked rows read `TICKS <habit>`; goal and theme rows keep `SERVES <x>`.
- **`FR-DESIGN-6.5` Cue label** `Cue · when X, I will do Y`, in the task editor and the habit editor.
- **`FR-DESIGN-6.6` Repeat** is the family word ("Repeat", "repeating", "Ends"). "Recurrence" and "recurring" leave the UI.
- **`FR-DESIGN-6.7` Edit scope labels** — **Just this one / This and every future one / The whole series** — everywhere a scope is asked: saving, deleting, thawing, closing, reopening.
- **`FR-DESIGN-6.8` "Scheduled" stays** in the All Tasks horizon line, where it means occurrences generated ahead, not a to-do date.
- **`FR-DESIGN-6.9` Code identifiers are not renamed** (`TaskState`, `dueDate`, `TaskState.BACKLOG`, `description`, …). Notification copy had no conflicts and is unchanged.

**Acceptance criteria**

### 3.4 The three-pane editor (`FR-EDITOR-1` — superseded and redefined)

*Source: [Editor frame: tab dots, opening tab, Cancel, new-task mode, retired sheets](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/7) (slides 1–3); scope at save from [When editor edits take effect, and when scope is asked](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/9) (slide 4). Built in M5 (frame) and M8 (scope at save, delete). **Supersedes** v2.11.0's `FR-EDITOR-1` and `FR-EDITOR-1.1`–`1.7` (the single-column field order and the `EditScope` banner above the title). The v2.11.0 user request behind it (`FR-2026-07-27-b`, alignment above the cue) is honoured inside the Alignment pane (`FR-EDITOR-6.1`).*

`FR-EDITOR-1` **The task editor is three panes — Task · Timing · Alignment — behind a tab row. Every field is edited in place; the only sheets left are scope at save, delete and discard. Changes are measured against the opening snapshot, and Save asks the edit scope once, only when it matters.** `FR-EDITOR-2`…`6` specify the diff and each pane; `FR-EDITOR-1` is the frame and the umbrella code they share.

- **`FR-EDITOR-1.1` The panes and their fields.**
  - **Task:** Title, Notes · optional, the Status strip (`FR-EDITOR-3`), Delete.
  - **Timing:** the shared calendar (`FR-EDITOR-4`) and the repeat card (`FR-EDITOR-5`).
  - **Alignment:** the habit it ticks, the goals it moves, the cue and the inherited theme line (`FR-EDITOR-6`).

  Until the pane milestones land, M5 places today's fields into their panes as they stand (dates and repeat on Timing; Serves, then Cue, on Alignment).
- **`FR-EDITOR-1.2` The tab row** sits on the kraft `surfaceAlt` layer between the rust title bar and the cream fields. The active tab is rust with a 3 dp rust underline; inactive tabs are muted ink. Every colour comes from `Tz` tokens, so all five palettes inherit it (`FR-DESIGN-4`). Tabs switch **by tap only** — no pager, no swipe, so they never compete with the rails and the calendar.
- **`FR-EDITOR-1.3` The editor opens on Task** from every entry point: a new task, a row, a quick-add expand, and the status sheet's Open editor. **The one exception is Reschedule** — the status sheet's Reschedule… and Planning's overdue Reschedule pill — which opens on **Timing with To do armed** (`FR-STATUS-1.9`, `FR-PLAN-4.5`; the route takes `tab=TIMING`).
- **`FR-EDITOR-1.4` The changed dot.** A tab shows a dot when that pane differs from the **opening snapshot** (the task as the editor opened it, presets included), per `FR-EDITOR-2`. No change, no dot; undoing an edit clears it; active and inactive tabs show it alike. The deck's always-on grey dots are not built.
- **`FR-EDITOR-1.5` Discard asks only when something changed.** Cancel, system Back and predictive back close at once with no changes; with changes, a Den sheet **"Discard your changes?"** offers **Keep editing** / **Discard**. An untouched quick-add expand closes silently, since its presets are in the snapshot.
- **`FR-EDITOR-1.6` The edit scope is asked at Save** (supersedes v2.11.0's `FR-EDITOR-1.6` banner). What Save does is one pure rule, `EditorSession.saveStep`:
  - **`FR-EDITOR-1.6.1` Close** — an existing task with no changes: the editor closes and writes nothing.
  - **`FR-EDITOR-1.6.2` Ask** — an existing **repeating** task with **field** changes (`DraftChanges.asksScope`): the **"This task repeats"** sheet opens.
  - **`FR-EDITOR-1.6.3` Write** under the default scope — everything else: a new task (untouched presets still save), a one-off, a one-off being turned into a repeating task (that creates a new series — nothing to scope), and a status-only save (it lands on this occurrence, `FR-REC-6.13`).
  - **`FR-EDITOR-1.6.4` The sheet** shows the "You changed" chips, then three radio rows — each with its label (`FR-DESIGN-6.7`), badge and consequence line (`FR-EDITOR-2.5`, `2.6`) — and **Keep editing** / **Save change**. The default is Just this one, or This and every future one when Just this one is disabled; the last choice isn't remembered. A disabled row is dimmed and can't be picked. The sheet rebuilds on each pick, because the `· this one only` suffix depends on the chosen row. Keep editing, Back and dragging the sheet down all keep the draft intact. Save change commits and closes the editor.
  - **`FR-EDITOR-1.6.5` A disabled scope never reaches the repository**: `saveDraft` throws on a scope the changes don't allow (`FR-REC-6.10`).
- **`FR-EDITOR-1.7` Save always looks enabled.** When something blocks it, Save switches to the first problem's pane and focuses that field — first a blank title (Task), then an incomplete cue time (Alignment, `FR-EDITOR-6.5`) — and nothing saves (`EditorSession.saveProblem`). This replaces the dimmed Save.
- **`FR-EDITOR-1.8` New-task mode** has the same three panes, is titled **NEW TASK**, and has no status strip and no Delete. It opens Open, or Backlog from the Backlog screen's expand (`FR-BACKLOG-5.2`). The title is the only required field; no scope is asked.
- **`FR-EDITOR-1.9` State survives.** The draft, the active tab and the opening snapshot survive rotation and process death (`EditorSessionCodec`, saved through `rememberSaveable`). Each pane keeps its own scroll position across tab switches.
- **`FR-EDITOR-1.10` Retired sheets.** The to-do date, deadline, end date, cue, habit and goal sheets retire (each is edited in place), and the editor's own status sheet gives way to the status strip. The editor keeps exactly three sheets: **scope at save**, **delete** and **discard**. `CueSheet` survives for the habit editor only.
- **`FR-EDITOR-1.11` Delete asks.**
  - A **repeating** task: **"Delete a repeating task"** — the same three rows with the delete lines and badges (`FR-EDITOR-2.7`), no "You changed" list, default Just this one, **Keep** and a red **Delete** (`Tz.colors.due` with cream text). It passes `ONE`, `FORWARD` or `ALL` to `deleteTask` (`FR-REC-6.14`).
  - A **one-off** task: **"Delete this task?"** with no options, **Keep** / **Delete**. There is no undo for delete, so one tap isn't enough.
  - Delete never asks "Discard your changes?" — the task is gone.
- **`FR-EDITOR-1.12` No `DM-NOT` surface.** Dots mean "changed", nothing else.

> **[OWNER TO CONFIRM]** (from M8) — the sheets' short intros: the save sheet's `Where should the change land?` (the deck's second sentence explained the design, not the task), the delete sheet's `What should go?`, and the one-off confirm's `It won't come back.`; and the `DenSheet` close × the slide doesn't show, which acts like Keep editing / Keep.

### 3.5 The change diff and the scope sheets' words (`FR-EDITOR-2`)

*Source: [The "You changed" diff and series counts](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/10) (slide 4). Built in M4 (`domain/editor/DraftDiff.kt`, `ScopeSheetCopy.kt`, `TaskRepository.countEarlierOccurrences`).*

`FR-EDITOR-2` **One pure, unit-tested comparison of the opening snapshot with the draft, as Save would store them, yields one entry per changed field — tagged with its pane and whether it's a repeat-card change. That single result drives the tab dots, the discard prompt, the scope sheet's "You changed" list and which scopes are allowed.**

- **`FR-EDITOR-2.1` What counts** (`DraftDiff.of(snapshot, draft, amountUnit)` → `DraftChanges`):

| Pane | Fields |
|---|---|
| Task | Title, Notes, Status (with the "How much?" amount when answered) |
| Timing | To-do date, Deadline, Repeat (type, days, "every N" stepper; turning repeat on or off is one entry), Ends, Deadline mode, the per-occurrence offset |
| Alignment | Cue, Habit, Goals |

  **Never counted:** the theme (read-only, inherited), `commitment` (no editor field), and identity fields (series, occurrence date).
- **`FR-EDITOR-2.2` Compared as stored.** The title is trimmed; goals compare as a set (reordering isn't a change); a rule's leftover fields from another pattern are ignored (a rule that means the same is no change). It is cheap enough to run on every keystroke.
- **`FR-EDITOR-2.3` One result, four uses:** `panes` → the dots (`FR-EDITOR-1.4`); `isEmpty` → the discard prompt (`FR-EDITOR-1.5`); `changes` → the "You changed" list; `touchesRepeatCard` / `repeatTurnedOff` → scope availability (`FR-REC-6.10`).
- **`FR-EDITOR-2.4` "You changed" wording.** One line per change, cut short if too long, wrapping across lines; **every change is listed**, with no "+N more". **Text values are never shown.**

| Field | Wording |
|---|---|
| Dates (to-do, deadline, ends) | `To-do date Thu 17 → Fri 18`. Missing values read **Undated** / **None** / **Never**. The month is added only when the dates fall in different months (`Wed 30 Sep → Fri 2 Oct`), and always against Undated / None / Never (`Undated → Fri 18 Sep`). Dates in different years both carry the year. |
| To-do date moved on a weekly or monthly repeat, under the forward or whole-series row | `To-do date Thu 17 → Fri 18 · this one only` |
| Status | `Status Open → Done`, or `Status Open → Done · 500 words` |
| Title, Notes | `Title changed`; `Notes added` / `changed` / `removed` |
| Cue, Habit | `Cue added` / `changed` / `removed`, and the same for Habit |
| Goals | `Goal added`, `2 goals added`, `Goal removed`; `Goals changed` when some are added and some removed |
| Repeat | `Repeat changed`, `Repeat turned on`, `Repeat turned off` (Ends, mode and offset aren't listed separately when repeat turns on or off) |
| Deadline mode | `Deadline One fixed → Per occurrence`; an old rule reads **Custom rule** |
| Offset | `Deadline +2 → +3 days each` |
| Repeating deadline set / cleared | `Deadline None → Fri 25 Sep`, `Deadline +3 days each → None` |

  A per-occurrence task's own deadline date follows its to-do date, so it isn't listed. A fixed deadline's date is a repeat-card change; the deadline of an occurrence on a Custom rule or no-deadline series is this occurrence's own date, so it isn't.
- **`FR-EDITOR-2.5` Badges** (`countEarlierOccurrences(taskId, notDoneOnly)`):

| Row | Save sheet | Delete sheet |
|---|---|---|
| Just this one | the occurrence's date (`17 SEP`) | the occurrence's date |
| This and every future one | none | none |
| The whole series | occurrences dated **before this one**, Done included | occurrences dated before this one **that aren't Done** |

  **A badge of 0 isn't shown, but both scope rows always show** — even on the first occurrence (the owner's call: people don't always notice they're on the first one). The count is what the whole-series row reaches beyond the forward row, which means the same for every series, including one with no end date.
- **`FR-EDITOR-2.6` Save sheet lines** (`ScopeSheetCopy.save`):

| Row | Line |
|---|---|
| Just this one | `Only Thu 17 Sep changes; the series stays as it is.` |
| Just this one, with a date move | `Friday moves; the series keeps its Tue & Thu rhythm.` (the **new** date's weekday; `its every-3-days rhythm`, `its 17th-of-the-month rhythm`, `its third-Thursday rhythm`, `Mon, Wed & Fri`, `daily`) |
| Just this one, disabled | `Repeat changes reach the series.` (also with repeat turned off) |
| This and every future one | `Edits forward in place. Past occurrences stay as they happened.` |
| This and every future one, repeat turned off | `This one becomes a one-off task; the series ends Wed 16 Sep.` |
| The whole series | `Rewrites every occurrence, past ones included, done ones too.` (count-free; the badge carries the number; disabled but shown with repeat turned off) |

  With a to-do date move under the forward or whole-series row, a second sentence follows: weekly or monthly — `The to-do date moves this one only.`; Every N days — `From Fri 18, every 3 days.` (`From Fri 18, every day.` at 1).
- **`FR-EDITOR-2.7` Delete sheet lines** (`ScopeSheetCopy.delete`):

| Row | Line |
|---|---|
| Just this one | `Removes Thu 17 Sep; it won't come back.` |
| This and every future one | `Removes this one and every later one. Done ones stay.` |
| The whole series | `Removes the series and every occurrence not done. Done ones stay as one-off tasks.` |

- **`FR-EDITOR-2.8` `ScopeSheetCopy.recountsOnMove(rule)`** — true only for Every N days — is the one predicate both the sheet text and `saveDraft` branch on (`FR-REC-6.11`), so they can't drift.

> **[DECISION]** (M4, easy to change) — a lone date carries its month; dates in different years both carry the year; "Repeat turned on" is one entry; "Friday moves" names the new date's weekday; the rhythm wordings above for monthly, three-plus weekdays and daily rules.

**Acceptance criteria**

1. An untouched draft has no changes; each field produces its entry on the right pane; undoing an edit clears it (`FR-EDITOR-2.1`). *`DraftDiffTest`*
2. Title whitespace, goal order and a same-meaning rule are not changes; theme, commitment and identity fields never count (`FR-EDITOR-2.1`, `2.2`). *`DraftDiffTest`*
3. Every wording row in `FR-EDITOR-2.4` — dates with month / year rules, status with amount, added / changed / removed, goals counts, repeat on / off / changed, deadline mode, offset, set / cleared, fixed-deadline move as a repeat-card change, the per-occurrence deadline not listed. *`DraftDiffTest`*
4. Entries follow pane order, Task then Timing then Alignment (`FR-EDITOR-2.1`). *`DraftDiffTest`*
5. The whole-series badge counts earlier occurrences (save) or earlier not-Done ones (delete); 0 for the first occurrence and a one-off; hidden at 0 while the row stays (`FR-EDITOR-2.5`). *`EarlierOccurrencesTest`, `ScopeSheetCopyTest`*
6. Save-sheet lines, disabled rows and defaults per `FR-EDITOR-2.6`, including the weekly / monthly "this one only" and the Every N days re-count sentences and the rhythm names (`FR-EDITOR-2.6`, `2.8`). *`ScopeSheetCopyTest`*

### 3.6 Edit and delete scopes (`FR-REC-6` — refined)

*Source: [When editor edits take effect, and when scope is asked](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/9) §5–§7 and its correction; [The "You changed" diff and series counts](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/10) §4, §8. Built in M6 (`DraftChanges.allows` / `defaultScope` / `asksScope`; `RoomTaskRepository.saveDraft` / `deleteTask`). Refines v2.9.0's `FR-REC-6` (date edits re-anchor the series under FORWARD / ALL) and the delete half of `FR-REC-4`.*

Facts that drove this, found in the code before the decisions: `saveDraft(ONE)` silently dropped repeat-rule changes; turning repeat off under ALL deleted the series **and every occurrence, Done history included**, and under ONE / FORWARD only detached the occurrence while the series kept running; weekly and monthly rules produce only their own days, so moving a Tue & Thu occurrence from Thu 17 to Fri 18 under FORWARD re-anchored the rule, deleted Thu 17 and never produced Fri 18; and deleting a Done task left its habit tick and goal progress pointing at nothing.

`FR-REC-6` **(refined) Which scopes a save may use depends on what changed; a to-do date move re-counts the rhythm only under Every N days; turning repeat off ends the series behind a one-off; and no delete removes a Done occurrence except deleting that occurrence itself.**

- **`FR-REC-6.10` Scope availability** — one rule (`DraftChanges.allows` / `defaultScope` / `asksScope`), shared by the sheet and `saveDraft`, which refuses (throws, writes nothing) a scope the changes don't allow:

| Change | Just this one | This and every future one | The whole series |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title, notes, cue, habit, goals | ✓ (default) | ✓ | ✓ — rewrites past occurrences too (this is how `DM-HABIT-8`'s "all occurrences incl. past" is chosen) |
| To-do date, or this occurrence's deadline | ✓ (default) | ✓ (`FR-REC-6.11`) | ✓ (`FR-REC-6.11`) |
| Anything on the repeat card (type, days, stepper, Ends, deadline mode, offset, fixed deadline) | disabled — "Repeat changes reach the series." | ✓ (default) | ✓ |
| Turning repeat off | disabled | **✓ (only option)** | disabled |

  No scope is asked when nothing changed or only the status did (`FR-EDITOR-1.6`).
- **`FR-REC-6.11` Moving the to-do date** (supersedes `FR-REC-6.1`–`6.3` and `6.5` for weekly and monthly rules):
  - **Weekly and monthly** (stepper above 1 included): only **this occurrence** moves, in every scope. It is marked edited; the rule's days and anchor never change. The scope's other edits still reach the chosen range.
  - **Every N days**, under the forward or whole-series scope: the rhythm **re-counts from the new date** (every 3 days from Thu 17, moved to Fri 18 → Fri 18, Mon 21, Thu 24 …) — `FR-REC-6.1`–`6.3` still describe this case. Under Just this one only the occurrence moves (`FR-REC-6.4`, unchanged).
  - **An edit that moves no date leaves the anchor alone** (supersedes v2.9.0 `FR-REC-6` acceptance 6, which re-anchored at this occurrence).
  - A changed one-off deadline on an occurrence stays on that occurrence.
- **`FR-REC-6.12` Turning repeat off** (forward only): this occurrence **keeps its id** and becomes a **one-off task** carrying the edits (so its habit and goal ledger rows stay linked); the series **ends the day before** its date; later occurrences that aren't Done are deleted; earlier ones are untouched; a later top-up brings nothing back. This replaces both the ALL collapse and the ONE / FORWARD detach.
- **`FR-REC-6.13` A status saved from the editor** applies **after** the field edits, through `setState(…, THIS_ONE)` with `draft.habitAmount`, to **this occurrence only**, whatever scope the field edits took — so habit ticks and goal moves are applied or reversed against the saved task. It follows the occurrence to its new id when an Every N days re-count regenerates it. A status-only save writes no fields, so the occurrence isn't marked edited. A save with no changes writes nothing.
- **`FR-REC-6.14` Delete scopes:**
  - **Just this one** (as before): the date becomes a series exception and never comes back.
  - **This and every future one (new):** the series ends the day before this occurrence; this and later occurrences that aren't Done are deleted; **Done ones are kept** — they already ticked habits and moved goals.
  - **The whole series:** deletes the series and every occurrence that isn't Done; **Done occurrences stay as one-off Done tasks** with no series. (Corrects the original "everything goes": deleting a series means "I don't want this any more", not "pretend I never did it".) A Done task you really want gone can still be deleted on its own.
- **`FR-REC-6.15` No `DM-NOT` surface.**

**Acceptance criteria**

1. Field and date edits allow every scope and start on Just this one; a repeat-card change rules out Just this one and starts on forward; turning repeat off allows forward only; no changes or a status alone asks nothing (`FR-REC-6.10`). *`ScopeAvailabilityTest`, `ScopeSheetCopyTest`*
2. A repeat-card change under Just this one is refused and nothing is written (`FR-REC-6.10`, `FR-EDITOR-1.6.5`). *`EditScopesTest`*
3. On a weekly rule a forward move shifts only this one while its other edits reach on; on a monthly rule a whole-series move shifts only this one; on Every N days a forward move re-counts from the new date (`FR-REC-6.11`). *`EditScopesTest`, `RecurringDateShiftTest`*
4. An edit that moves no date leaves the anchor alone (`FR-REC-6.11`). *`RecurringDateShiftTest`*
5. Turning repeat off makes this one a one-off and ends the series the day before (`FR-REC-6.12`). *`EditScopesTest`, `ScopeSheetsTest`*
6. A status waiting in the draft applies after the field edits, with its amount; it reaches only this occurrence whatever the scope; alone it leaves the occurrence unedited; it follows a re-counted occurrence; no changes writes nothing (`FR-REC-6.13`). *`EditScopesTest`*
7. Deleting forward ends the series the day before and keeps Done ones; deleting the whole series keeps Done ones as one-off tasks; deleting just this one leaves the rest (`FR-REC-6.14`). *`EditScopesTest`*

### 3.7 The Task pane and its status strip (`FR-EDITOR-3`)

*Source: [When editor edits take effect, and when scope is asked](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/9) §1–§4, [The new status sheet](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/11) §7 (slide 1). Built in M9 (`EditorSession.chip` / `status` / `toDo`). Supersedes the editor's state row, which applied a status at once through `onSetState` and ignored the draft's status at Save.*

`FR-EDITOR-3` **On the Task pane, five status chips pick into the draft. A status that changes only this task waits for Save like any other edit; a status that reaches a series opens the status sheet's scope panel, and a whole-series choice acts at once and closes the editor.**

- **`FR-EDITOR-3.1` Draft semantics.** A single-task status — a one-off task, or an occurrence going Open, Done, Closed (this one), a single-occurrence thaw, or Backlog — lights the Task dot, appears in "You changed", is thrown away by Discard, shows **no toast**, and applies last at Save (`FR-REC-6.13`). Going back to the status the editor opened with is just a draft change and never asks.
- **`FR-EDITOR-3.2` Chips that need a scope** — Frozen on a repeating task (Freeze series), Closed on a repeating task, and Open / Done / Closed on an occurrence of a frozen series (the thaw) — open the status sheet **directly at its scope panel** (`FR-STATUS-1.6`). "Just this one" goes into the draft. **A whole-series choice acts at once** (through `StatusChanges.change(…, WHOLE_SERIES)`, with the no-Undo toast, `FR-STATUS-4.1`) and **closes the editor** — after "Discard your changes?" if the draft has changes, so a stale draft can't undo the series change. Back closes the sheet and the chip keeps its earlier status.
- **`FR-EDITOR-3.3` Open and Done on a repeating task** just select, since they reach only this occurrence.
- **`FR-EDITOR-3.4` "How much?" at the Done chip.** A task that ticks an amount-counting habit or moves an amount-adding goal (`AmountPrompt.of`, the same rule the lists use) asks "How much?" when Done is tapped. The amount waits in `TaskDraft.habitAmount` and applies at Save; cancelling leaves the chip as it was; if the links change later in the draft, the prompt is asked again only when the new links need it. This fixes the old editor path, which marked Done without an amount.
- **`FR-EDITOR-3.5` Status and dates stay consistent in the draft** (this is `FR-BACKLOG-5.6`, extending `FR-BACKLOG-5`'s "unless the user … adds a toDoDate" into the editor draft):
  - the **Backlog** chip clears the to-do date, so Timing shows the task undated;
  - **picking a to-do date on a Backlog draft makes it Open**;
  - **Undated** leaves an Open task Open — it never sends a task to Backlog;
  - **Frozen** and **Closed** keep their dates.
- **`FR-EDITOR-3.6` Backlog on a repeating task** is dimmed in the strip, with the hint under it: "Backlog is unavailable while this repeats — freeze it instead." (moved from the Timing pane, slide 1).
- **`FR-EDITOR-3.7` New-task mode has no strip** (`FR-EDITOR-1.8`).

**Acceptance criteria**

1. A chip picks into the draft and lights the Task dot; Done carries its amount; going back to the opening status is a plain draft change (`FR-EDITOR-3.1`, `3.4`). *`EditorSessionTest`*
2. A chip that reaches the series opens the scope panel directly, with no five-row list and no footer; Back keeps the draft; Freeze series acts (`FR-EDITOR-3.2`). *`EditorSessionTest`, `StatusSheetFlowTest`*
3. With a changed draft, a whole-series choice asks "Discard your changes?" first, then acts and closes the editor (`FR-EDITOR-3.2`). **TEST GAP** — device-checked in M9 only.
4. The Backlog chip clears the to-do date, and a to-do date brings a Backlog draft back to Open (`FR-EDITOR-3.5`). *`EditorSessionTest`*
5. Cancelling "How much?" at the Done chip leaves the chip as it was (`FR-EDITOR-3.4`). **TEST GAP**
6. On a repeating task the Backlog chip is dimmed with its hint under the strip (`FR-EDITOR-3.6`). **TEST GAP**

### 3.8 The shared calendar on the Timing pane (`FR-EDITOR-4`)

*Source: [Shared calendar: armed field, quick picks, window, conflict fix](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/4) and its handoff from [Repeat settings inline in the Timing pane](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/5) (slides 3, 5, 6). The rules are built in M10 (`domain/editor/SharedCalendar.kt`, `domain/dates/MonthGrid.kt`); the pane is built in M15 ([Implement: shared calendar in the Timing pane](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/27), `ui/editor/TimingCalendar.kt`). Replaces the editor's To Do Date and Due Date sheets. (M10's commit cites the umbrella `FR-EDITOR-1`; this is its own code.)*

`FR-EDITOR-4` **The to-do date and the deadline are set on one calendar. Two tiles say which field a tap sets (the armed field); the rail, the grid marks, the caption and the conflict warning all follow it. The rules are pure (`SharedCalendar(draft, armed, today)`, each change returning a new draft); the pane only renders them.**

- **`FR-EDITOR-4.1` Layout** (slide 3, middle): the `DATES · ONE CALENDAR, TWO FIELDS` card — the **To-do** and **Deadline** tiles with the armed one lit; the caption ("A TAP SETS TO-DO" / "… THE DEADLINE"); the armed field's rail; the month header with ‹ ›; the grid with its marks; the legend `TO-DO · DEADLINE · THE WINDOW BETWEEN · TODAY` (plus `END` while an end date shows); a hint under it that follows the armed field (`SharedCalendar.hint`, e.g. "Tapping Thu 17 again clears the to-do date. The task becomes undated and leaves Today."); and the inline conflict warning, placed under the tiles. Eyebrows, caption and legend use the hyphenated `TO-DO` (`FR-DESIGN-6.3`), not the deck's "TO DO".
- **`FR-EDITOR-4.2` The armed field.** **To do is always armed when the Timing pane opens**, including on a task with only a deadline, and on Reschedule (`FR-EDITOR-1.3`). It changes only when the other tile is tapped — picking a date never moves it. A third, temporary **End** mode is entered only from the repeat card (`FR-EDITOR-4.14`). The armed field and the shown month are remembered until the editor closes, across tab switches and rotation.
- **`FR-EDITOR-4.3` The month** opens on the to-do date's month, else the deadline's, else this month (`SharedCalendar.openingMonth`). **Switching tiles never changes the month.** The arrows go to any month, past or future.
- **`FR-EDITOR-4.4` The to-do rail:** `Today · Tomorrow · <weekday of today+2> · Undated`. The third chip is always two days out (the deck's "Sat" is just its example), so it never repeats Today or Tomorrow and doesn't depend on the week start. The chip matching the current value is highlighted.
- **`FR-EDITOR-4.5` The deadline rail:** `<current deadline, e.g. "Fri 25"> · +1 week · +2 weeks · None`. The first chip is highlighted and inert. **+1 / +2 weeks count from the to-do date, or from today when undated**, so a chip always gives the same window. With no deadline the first chip is left out and None is highlighted. A highlighted or disabled chip does nothing.
- **`FR-EDITOR-4.6` Tap again to clear.** Tapping the armed field's own date clears it — for the to-do date, the deadline and the end date. When two dates share a day, a tap clears only the armed one.
- **`FR-EDITOR-4.7` Marks** (`mark(day)`): a filled day for To do, a ringed day for the deadline, the days **strictly between** shaded (the window), a dot for today, and a short bar under the end date. Both dates on one day draw filled and ringed. Both marks always draw, whichever field is armed, past dates and a window that ended included. Past days can be picked, aren't dimmed and get no warning. **No window is shaded when the deadline comes before the to-do date.**
- **`FR-EDITOR-4.8` Deadline before the to-do date.** The warning "You plan to work on this after it's due." shows only when both dates are set and the deadline is **strictly** earlier; it never blocks Save. **The one-tap fix moves the to-do date to the deadline day.** When the deadline is already past, the warning stays but the fix is hidden — the honest fix there is to move the deadline.
- **`FR-EDITOR-4.9` Backlog and undated.** A Backlog task's To do tile reads **Backlog**, with no chip highlighted; picking a date or a chip makes it Open with that date at Save (`FR-BACKLOG-5.6`). **Undated on a Backlog task does nothing.** Undated on an Open task keeps it Open and undated (it waits in Planning). A Backlog task can have a deadline, and setting one doesn't change its status.
- **`FR-EDITOR-4.10` A repeating task.** The calendar marks **only the occurrence being edited**. A to-do tap moves that occurrence, even to a day off the rule (how far it reaches is `FR-REC-6.11`). On a new repeating task the to-do date sets the first occurrence, the rule's anchor. **Undated and tap-again-to-clear are off for the to-do date**; the caption says "A repeating task always has a to-do date."
- **`FR-EDITOR-4.11` Per-occurrence deadline mode.** The Deadline tile reads e.g. `Thu 15 · +2 days each`. A tap **sets the offset** to the gap between the tapped day and the to-do date, capped at 30; days before the to-do date are dimmed and can't be tapped. The rail is the same shape and its picks set the offset (+1 week = 7). Moving this occurrence's to-do date moves its deadline too, so the offset holds; the deadline draws as to-do + offset even when the draft has no `dueDate`. So the conflict warning only ever comes up with a fixed deadline.
- **`FR-EDITOR-4.12` The first deadline on a repeating task is a fixed one** (`SINGULAR`). Clearing any repeating deadline leaves none, and a per-occurrence one also loses its offset.
- **`FR-EDITOR-4.13` An old custom deadline rule** (`RECURRING`) is read-only on the calendar: in Deadline mode every day and chip is dimmed until it's switched to an offset (`FR-EDITOR-5.8`), and it never gets the conflict warning.
- **`FR-EDITOR-4.14` End mode** (entered from the repeat card's Ends → On a date): caption "A TAP SETS THE END"; rail `+1 month · +3 months · end of year · Never`, counted from the to-do date, where **end of year is 31 Dec of the to-do date's own year** (even in December); days before the to-do date dimmed; tapping the end date again clears it to Never.
- **`FR-EDITOR-4.15` Tiles use the short form** (`Tue 13`, `Thu 15 · +2 days each`), with `Undated` / `Backlog` / `None`. A to-do date of today leads with it, as on slide 5: `Today · Sat 10`.
- **`FR-EDITOR-4.16` The grid follows the week-start setting** through `MonthGrid.of(month, weekStart)` (`FR-SET-1.1`).
- **`FR-EDITOR-4.17` Edits go through the draft,** so the tab dot, "You changed" and the scope at save keep working. The editor's `"todo"` / `"due"` sheets and the old `DateRow`s retire; Reschedule's interim "pop the old picker" goes.
- **`FR-EDITOR-4.18` Accessible days.** Every grid day has a TalkBack label naming the day and its marks, e.g. "Thursday 17 September, to-do date" (`NFR-A11Y-1.5`). Colours come from `Tz` tokens in all five palettes.

### 3.9 The repeat card on the Timing pane (`FR-EDITOR-5`)

*Source: [Repeat settings inline in the Timing pane](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/5) (slides 3, 5). The model is built in M11 ([Domain: repeat card model (pure)](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/22), `domain/editor/RepeatCard.kt`); the pane is planned in [Implement: inline repeat card in the Timing pane](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/29). (M11's commit cites the umbrella `FR-EDITOR-1`; this is its own code.) Replaces the editor's recurrence block, the "Recurring deadline" rule builder and the end-date sheet. Refines `FR-BACKLOG-5.3`.*

`FR-EDITOR-5` **Repeat is edited inline in one card under the calendar, with no modal sheets. Its three types map one-to-one onto the rule patterns the app already supports, so nothing that could be set before is lost.**

- **`FR-EDITOR-5.1` Layout.** A header row (↻ the repeat's summary, and the on/off toggle); a `Weekly · Monthly · Every N days` segment with that type's body; a footer row with two halves, **Ends** and **Deadline**.
- **`FR-EDITOR-5.2` Types:**

| Segment | Rule pattern | Body |
|---|---|---|
| **Weekly** | `WEEKDAY` | weekday chips, plus an "every [1] week" stepper (at 1 it reads as plain weekly) |
| **Monthly** | `MONTHDAY` | "On the 17th / On the 3rd Thursday", plus an "every [1] month" stepper |
| **Every N days** | `INTERVAL`, unit `DAY` | an "every [N] days" stepper |

  Every N weeks is Weekly with its stepper; every N months is Monthly with its stepper; **daily** is Every 1 day. **Yearly stays out** of this version.
- **`FR-EDITOR-5.3` Switching type** gives a default rule for the new type anchored on the to-do date — its weekday, its day of the month, its nth weekday (a fifth weekday becomes "last") — and carries the "every N" step. So Weekly → Monthly → Weekly drops weekdays picked earlier. Picking the type already shown changes nothing (which is how an old rule stays read-only). The last weekday chip of a Weekly rule can't be turned off — a rule with no days makes no occurrences.
- **`FR-EDITOR-5.4` Old interval rules are read-only.** A series saved as `INTERVAL` with unit `WEEK` or `MONTH` opens under Every N days showing its real unit, read-only, until another type is picked. Its rule is never rewritten silently.
- **`FR-EDITOR-5.5` The summary.** Weekly abbreviates one or two days ("Every Tue & Thu", "Every Mon"); three or more days and the other types read as `Recurrence.summary` does ("The 17th of every month", "Every 3 days"). `Recurrence.summary` itself is unchanged, since the rows and the status sheet use it. The footer halves read `Ends never` / `on Thu 31 Dec` / `at the deadline` and `Deadline none` / `one fixed` / `+2 days each` / `custom rule`; the UI draws the `›`.
- **`FR-EDITOR-5.6` Ends and Deadline swap the card body in place;** a "‹ Repeat" link goes back to the rule.
- **`FR-EDITOR-5.7` Ends:** `Never · On a date`. On a date arms the calendar's End mode (`FR-EDITOR-4.14`). **With a fixed deadline** the half reads "Ends at the deadline" and can't be tapped — the end date *is* the deadline.
- **`FR-EDITOR-5.8` Deadline:** `One fixed · Per occurrence`; Per occurrence shows a 0–30 offset stepper (`FR-REC-4.1`). With no deadline the half reads **"Deadline none ›"**, and tapping it arms the calendar's Deadline tile — you set a date first, then choose a mode; the first one is One fixed (`FR-EDITOR-4.12`). An old `RECURRING` series shows **"Custom deadline rule"**, read-only, with **Switch to offset** (`FR-REC-4.4`), which also sets this occurrence's deadline to its to-do date plus the offset. The footer always keeps both halves. Switching modes:
  - **One fixed → Per occurrence** keeps the gap between the to-do date and the deadline as the offset (held to 0–30) and moves this occurrence's deadline to match;
  - **Per occurrence → One fixed** keeps this occurrence's deadline as the date **and clears the end date**, because the fixed deadline is the end — a stale earlier end date would otherwise cut the series short silently;
  - the offset stepper holds 0–30 and moves this occurrence's deadline with it.
- **`FR-EDITOR-5.9` Folding.** While the calendar's Deadline tile is armed — unless the Deadline body is open — the card folds into one row: the summary plus the deadline mode, `Every Tue & Thu · ONE FIXED DEADLINE`, `… · DEADLINE +2 DAYS EACH`, `… · CUSTOM DEADLINE RULE`, or `… · NO DEADLINE` (inert: the tile is already armed). It unfolds when To do is armed again; tapping the folded row opens the Deadline body. The callout shows **only for One fixed**.
- **`FR-EDITOR-5.10` Turning repeat on and off** (refines `FR-BACKLOG-5.3`):
  - on a **Backlog** draft: Open with today's to-do date (`FR-BACKLOG-5.3`, kept);
  - on an **undated Open** draft: today's to-do date is set in the draft (**new** — the calendar shows where the series starts; before, it stayed undated and anchored to today only at save);
  - on a dated draft that already has a deadline: the deadline becomes **One fixed** (the first deadline defaults to fixed);
  - the new rule is anchored on the to-do date;
  - off: the rule is cleared from the draft only; what that does to an existing series is `FR-REC-6.12`.

  The grey hint "Backlog is for non-recurring tasks — a recurring series can't be 'someday'" is **dropped** (the status sheet and the strip explain it, `FR-STATUS-1.8`, `FR-EDITOR-3.6`), and the old `toggleRecurrence` is replaced by the domain rule.
- **`FR-EDITOR-5.11` Repeat-card changes still disable Just this one** at save (`FR-REC-6.10`).
- **`FR-EDITOR-5.12` Accessible controls.** The steppers, the segment and the body swap carry TalkBack labels; colours come from `Tz` tokens (`NFR-A11Y-1`).
- **`FR-EDITOR-5.13` Calendar taps stay the calendar's.** Setting or clearing a deadline date, the first deadline being One fixed, and a per-occurrence tap setting the offset are `SharedCalendar`'s rules (`FR-EDITOR-4.11`, `4.12`); the card doesn't repeat them.

> **[OWNER TO CONFIRM]** (from M11) — the callout's wording, `One fixed deadline also ends the series. For a fresh deadline each time, switch to per occurrence.` (written to match the deck's gist), and the rulings in `FR-EDITOR-5.3`, `5.5`, `5.8` and `5.9` above.

The pane must clear `endDate` when a calendar tap creates the first fixed deadline, or route that tap through `RepeatCard.deadlineKind`'s rule, so the two seams agree.

**Acceptance criteria**

### 3.10 The Alignment pane (`FR-EDITOR-6`)

*Source: [Alignment pane: inline cue, one habit, any goals, inherited theme](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/6) (slide 3, right). The rules are built in M12 ([Domain: Alignment pane rules (pure)](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/23): `domain/editor/CueDraft.kt`, `CueSuggestions.kt`, `AlignmentLists.kt`, `InheritedTheme.kt`); the pane is built in M16 ([Implement: inline Alignment pane](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/28), `ui/editor/AlignmentPane.kt`). Replaces the editor's cue, habit and goal sheets. Refines `DM-TASK-4` (the cue prompt's wording) and `DM-TASK-5` (the direct theme link). `FR-CUE-1`…`3` carry.*

`FR-EDITOR-6` **The Alignment pane edits the cue, the one habit the task ticks and the goals it moves in place, and shows — read-only — the themes the task inherits from them.**

- **`FR-EDITOR-6.1` Sections, in this order:** `Cue · when X, I will do Y`; `Ticks a habit · one`; `Moves a goal · any`; the inherited theme line at the bottom. **The cue comes first, as on slide 3 (right).** The owner chose this when building #28, and it supersedes the alignment-above-cue order that v2.11.0's `FR-EDITOR-1` took from `FR-2026-07-27-b`.
- **`FR-EDITOR-6.2` The cue, inline:** the `After a routine · At a time · At a place` segment; a label field with ✎ / × (× clears the cue); an inline `HH:mm` time field for At a time and After a routine, typed with no modal picker; and suggestion chips — tapping one fills the field.
- **`FR-EDITOR-6.3` Suggestions** (`CueSuggestions`) are the user's own past cue labels of the selected type, from both tasks and habits, deduplicated (trimmed, ignoring case, keeping the first spelling) and most-used first, ties in the order first met. A habit, a one-off task or a whole series each counts once — a daily series is one use, not one per occurrence. Under At a time they are previously used valid times from At a time cues only. With fewer than three, the per-type starters **top up** the list after the user's own; the row stops at **six** chips.
- **`FR-EDITOR-6.4` Fields per type, and only the selected type's are saved:**
  - **At a time:** the time is required, the label optional (`FR-CUE-2`'s label-less time cue carries, displaying as the time alone, `FR-CUE-2.3`, `FR-CUE-3`).
  - **After a routine:** the label is required; **a time is now optional** and reminds at that time. The reminder pipeline already honoured it (`FR-NOTIF-1`, `NotifyLogic.remindersFor`); only the old sheet never offered the field.
  - **At a place:** a label only (`FR-NOTIF-3`). A stray time left under At a place is dropped — fixing the old quirk that saved it.

  Typed text persists while switching type (`FR-CUE-1.6`). A cue exists only when its type's required field is filled. `CueTime.isValid` is now the one `HH:mm` rule, and `CueSheet` delegates to it.
- **`FR-EDITOR-6.5` An incomplete time blocks Save.** A time field holding text that isn't a valid `HH:mm`, under a type that shows it, yields `SaveProblem.INCOMPLETE_CUE_TIME` (after `BLANK_TITLE`, `FR-EDITOR-1.7`; `EditorSession.saveProblem(cue: CueDraft)`): Save switches to Alignment, selects that cue type, focuses the time field and marks it incomplete. An empty time is never incomplete. A stored time cue that isn't zero-padded (e.g. `8:30`) also shows the problem, so the user fixes it rather than losing the cue silently.
- **`FR-EDITOR-6.6` Ticks a habit · one.** A radio list of **all** habits (habits have no archived state), in Habits-tab order. **Tapping the selected habit deselects it** (no "No habit" row). The subtitle is the habit's **static cadence** in the Habits tab's words — `3× / WEEK`, `10 PAGES / DAY`, `DAILY` for 7×/week — with no streak and no weekly progress (`DEC-2`, `DM-NOT`, §7).
- **`FR-EDITOR-6.7` Moves a goal · any.** A checkbox list of the **ongoing goals, plus any non-ongoing goal the task is linked to now or was linked to when the editor opened** — so an unticked one can be re-ticked until Save, and is gone for good after it. Subtitles by type: Accumulative `62,000 / 80,000 WORDS`; Stepped `3 OF 5 STEPS`; Generic its deadline (`BY 12 DEC`, the year shown when it differs) or nothing — **never `Goal.pct()`'s placeholder 40 %** (§7). A non-ongoing goal's subtitle leads with its status: `COMPLETED · 62,000 / 80,000 WORDS`, `FROZEN · 3 OF 5 STEPS`, or just `COMPLETED`. Numbers group with `Locale.US` (`62,000`), as in the deck.
- **`FR-EDITOR-6.8` The theme is inherited, read-only** (refines `DM-TASK-5`). The line lists the primary themes of the linked goals (in link order) then the habit, deduplicated, naming only the links that actually bring a theme: "Theme **A** — inherited from the goal" / "…the habit" / "…the goal and the habit", or "Themes **A** · **B** — inherited from the goals" (/ "…the goals and the habit"); links to anything deleted are skipped; with nothing linked, a faint "No theme yet — it comes from a goal". TalkBack reads it as one sentence. **`Task.themeId` is ignored by the editor** (not shown, not edited) but carried untouched (§2). Its only writer, `DirectionsViewModel.quickAddTask`, has no caller and is deleted.
- **`FR-EDITOR-6.9` The pane scrolls** when lists are long — no cap, no "Show all". Empty lists keep today's copy in the new words (e.g. "No habits yet — start one in the Habits tab.").
- **`FR-EDITOR-6.10` A quick-add expand's parsed cue** (`FR-CAPTURE-3.6`) shows pre-filled on the Alignment pane **and lights its dot**. The owner chose this when building #28. The parsed cue is an edit, not a preset (`EditorSession.open(…, parsedCue)`), so Cancel on an untouched expand with a parsed cue asks "Discard your changes?". Other presets (title, Today, Backlog) stay in the snapshot, and an expand with only those still closes silently (`FR-EDITOR-1`).
- **`FR-EDITOR-6.11` The habit editor keeps `CueSheet`.** It isn't redesigned; it may share the suggestion source.

> **[OWNER TO CONFIRM]** (from M12) — the starter chips: After a routine `After coffee`, `After the school run`, `After dinner` (from the deck); At a time `07:00`, `12:30`, `18:00`; At a place `At home`, `At my desk`, `At the gym`. Also the top-up (not replace) rule, the six-chip limit, times offered only from At a time cues, the status-led subtitle of a non-ongoing goal, the mixed-source theme sentence and the `Locale.US` grouping (the goal cards use the device locale).
- **`FR-EDITOR-6.12` RTL by content.** Habit, goal and cue labels render by their own direction (`contentDir()`, `FR-DESIGN-3`). Colours come from `Tz` tokens; TalkBack reads each habit and goal row with its subtitle and state (`NFR-A11Y-1.7`).

### 3.11 Every calendar follows the week start (`FR-SET-1.1`, refines `FR-SET-1` / `DM-REL-2`)

*Source: [Shared calendar: armed field, quick picks, window, conflict fix](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/4) ("Week start and the other pickers"). Grid built in M10; the pickers in M14 ([Implement: week start in every calendar picker](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/26)).*

Fact found on the map: `CalendarPicker` always drew a Sunday-first grid (`Dates.dayOfWeek` is `% 7`), although Settings offers Sunday, Monday or Saturday.

`FR-SET-1.1` **The week-start setting (`FR-SET-1`, `DM-REL-2`) also orders every calendar grid — its weekday header and its leading blanks — in the editor's shared calendar and in `CalendarPicker` (Backlog pull, habit log date, Planning's "Plan through" range, the theme editor). ### 3.12 The status sheet (`FR-STATUS-1`)

*Source: [The new status sheet](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/11), with constraints from [Closing or freezing one occurrence vs the whole series](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/2) and [Wording: deck terms vs app terms, app-wide](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/3) (slides 1, 7–10). Built in M9 (`domain/status/StatusSheet.kt`, `ui/status/TaskStatusSheet.kt`). Replaces `StateSheet` and its guard panes; refines `FR-BACKLOG-2` and `FR-PLAN-4`.*

`FR-STATUS-1` **One status sheet shows all five statuses and what each one does. A pick that changes just this task applies at once with an Undo toast; a pick that reaches a series turns the sheet into a scope panel, and nothing applies until its button is pressed.**

- **`FR-STATUS-1.1` One sheet, every entry point.** `TaskStatusSheet` is reached by holding a list checkbox (`FR-STATUS-3`), by a tap on a Closed, Frozen or Backlog task (`FR-STATUS-2`), by TalkBack's "Change status", by All Tasks' row long-press, by Planning's **Set status** pill, and — at the scope panel only — from the editor's strip (`FR-EDITOR-3.2`). `StateSheet.kt` and the guard panes `ConfirmFreezeSeries` and `RecurringCannotBacklog` are deleted.
- **`FR-STATUS-1.2` The header** shows the task's checkbox in its current status (not tappable), the title, and the meta line `<to-do date, or NO DATE> · <repeat summary, if it repeats> · <the row's alignment label>` — the alignment label is the same `TICKS <habit>` the list row shows. Then `SET STATUS`, plus `· this task repeats` when it does. The deck's `CAPTURED …` and `DOESN'T REPEAT` are dropped.
- **`FR-STATUS-1.3` The rows,** in the deck's order — **Done, Open, Closed, Frozen, Backlog** — each with one description (one set for one-off and repeating tasks):

| Status | Description |
|---|---|
| Done | built from the links: "Counts toward *<habit>* and *<goal>*." (habit first, then goals; past two, "*A*, *B* and N more"); with none, "Finished. Files into Journey." |
| Open | "Back on your list." |
| Closed | "No longer relevant. Kept in history, never counted as a miss." |
| Frozen | "On hold and hidden from the calendar until you thaw it." — on a repeating task, "Freezes the whole series until you thaw it." |
| Backlog | "Off the calendar, into the backlog." |

  The current status's row carries a **`NOW`** badge (not `CURRENT`). The footnote reads "Nothing here is a failure.".
- **`FR-STATUS-1.4` Apply at once, or ask** (`StatusSheet.pick` → `Apply` / `AskScope` / `Stay`, built on `StateMachine.askFor`). A button appears only when a scope must be asked or the change can't be undone. A pick that changes only this task — any status on a one-off, and any change to an occurrence that needs no scope — **applies as soon as the row is tapped**; the sheet closes and the Undo toast shows (`FR-STATUS-4`). The deck's "Move to Someday" button is dropped.
- **`FR-STATUS-1.5` The scope panel** replaces the list when a pick needs a scope — Freeze series, Closed on a repeating task, a thaw, a reopen of a closed series. It shows the header, the chosen row alone with ✓, the panel, and a footer **[Back] [<action>]** (Reschedule… and Open editor hide). It defaults to **Just this one**; the action button's label follows the selected choice; Back returns to the list with nothing applied. **No count badge** (the explanation carries the meaning; counts stay on the save sheet), and the deck's grey "Done and Open apply to the occurrence…" note is dropped.

| Pick | Just this one | The whole series | Whole-series line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Closed | **Close this one** | **Close series** | "Ends the series today. Past ones not done are closed; later ones stop appearing." |
| Thaw → Open | **Mark open** | **Thaw series** | "Occurrences come back from today. The days it spent frozen stay gone." |
| Thaw → Done | **Mark done** | **Thaw series** (this one done) | as Thaw → Open |
| Thaw → Closed | **Close this one** | **Close series** | as Closed |
| Reopen → Open / Done | **Mark open** / **Mark done** | **Reopen series** | "Occurrences come back from today. Past ones are never changed." |
| Freeze (no choice) | — | **Freeze series** | "No occurrences until you thaw it. This one stays, frozen." |

  Just this one always reads "Only this occurrence changes." The action button takes the target's colour (Frozen blue-grey as the deck shows, Closed in the closed tone, otherwise rust).
- **`FR-STATUS-1.6` From the editor** the sheet opens **directly at the panel** — no five-row list, no Reschedule… / Open editor. Back closes it and the chip keeps its earlier status; Just this one goes into the draft; a whole-series choice acts at once and closes the editor (`FR-EDITOR-3.2`).
- **`FR-STATUS-1.7` The current status's row** does nothing when tapped, with two exceptions: **Frozen** on a repeating task whose series isn't frozen opens the Freeze series panel; **Closed** on a repeating task whose series hasn't ended opens the Close series panel, with Just this one greyed (it's already true) and the whole series chosen. An occurrence thawed alone, whose series is still frozen, freezes alone without asking.
- **`FR-STATUS-1.8` Backlog on a repeating task** (this is `FR-BACKLOG-2.1`, refining `FR-BACKLOG-2`: a dimmed row replaces the guard pane): the row is shown dimmed with the inline tag `REPEATS — USE FREEZE`, and tapping it does nothing. The footnote reads "Backlog is unavailable while this repeats. Freeze it instead, or turn the repeat off in the editor." before "Nothing here is a failure.". There is no turn-repeat-off shortcut — turning repeat off has its own scope, asked at Save (`FR-REC-6.12`).
- **`FR-STATUS-1.9` The footer from a list** is **[Reschedule…] [Open editor]**, for every status. Open editor opens the editor on Task. **Reschedule… opens the editor on Timing with To do armed** (`FR-EDITOR-1.3`); a rescheduled date applies only at Save, a repeating task asks the usual scope, Cancel throws it away, and no toast appears.
  - **`FR-PLAN-4.5`** (refines `FR-PLAN-4`): Planning's overdue pills read `Today · Reschedule · Set status`. **Reschedule** opens the editor on Timing with To do armed, like the sheet's Reschedule…; **Set status** opens this sheet. Planning's "Move it to" calendar sheet and `PlanningViewModel.rescheduleTo` are removed. The overdue checkbox follows `FR-STATUS-2` / `FR-STATUS-3` like every other list checkbox.
- **`FR-STATUS-1.10` "How much?" before Done.** Done picked in the sheet on an amount task asks first, as a tap does; the toast follows the confirmed amount; cancelling changes nothing.
- **`FR-STATUS-1.11` Backlog on a one-off** clears its to-do date (`FR-BACKLOG-2`), and Undo restores it.
- **`FR-STATUS-1.12` No `DM-NOT` surface.** No count badge, no streak; "Nothing here is a failure." stays.

> **[OWNER TO CONFIRM]** (from M9) — the panel headings `CLOSE WHAT?`, `THAW WHAT?`, `REOPEN WHAT?` and `FREEZE THE SERIES` (the deck shows only "FREEZE WHAT?"); the meta line showing only `TICKS <habit>` (a goal-only task shows no label, since a task *moves* a goal and the row shows none); the repeat summary reading `EVERY TUESDAY & THURSDAY` (`Recurrence.summary`, as on the rows) where the deck has `TUE & THU`, which wraps the meta to two lines; the action button's colours; and TalkBack's "Unavailable while this repeats. ### 3.13 The tap rule, the hold gesture and the undo toast (`FR-STATUS-2`, `FR-STATUS-3`, `FR-STATUS-4`; refines `FR-AUDIO-1.2`)

*Source: [Hold-to-choose gesture and undo toast on list rows](https://github.com/DanielNakash/Tzafon/issues/8) (slides 7, 8, 11). Built in M7 (`domain/status/TapRule.kt`, `StatusChanges.kt`, `StatusToastCopy.kt`, `StatusUndo.kt`; `ui/components/HoldCheckbox.kt`; `ui/status/StatusToastHost.kt`). Facts checked first: taps behaved differently per list; Also today's row long-press started a reorder, checkbox included; the app had no toast and no haptics.*

`FR-STATUS-2` **One tap rule for every list checkbox: Open ↔ Done; Closed, Frozen and Backlog open the status sheet.**

- **`FR-STATUS-2.1` Where:** every checkbox that already responded to taps — Today (Focus, Also today, This week's priorities, the Done strip), Planning (normal and overdue rows), All Tasks and Backlog. **No new checkboxes** on Journey, Habits or goal detail.
- **`FR-STATUS-2.2` The tap:** an Open task becomes Done; a Done task becomes Open (**mark open** — "reopen" is kept for a closed series). Closed, Frozen and Backlog **open the status sheet** — a tap never guesses a status change. This changes Today and Planning (which toggled everything); Backlog (which always opened the sheet) is unchanged in effect.
- **`FR-STATUS-2.3` Amount tasks** (`DM-HABIT-5`, `DM-GOAL-3`) still ask "How much?" first; the toast follows the confirmed amount; cancelling changes nothing and shows no toast.
- **`FR-STATUS-2.4` One rule in one place** (`TapRule`) replaces the per-screen `requestToggle` copies, the view models' `toggleDone` / `setState` and the triplicated chime code.

`FR-STATUS-3` **Holding a list checkbox for 350 ms raises the status sheet.**

- **`FR-STATUS-3.1` The hold** (custom 350 ms, not Android's 400 ms): the sheet rises **at the threshold**, finger still down, with one light haptic tick, the box scaling to 1.16 and a ripple growing over the hold. Releasing earlier is a tap; moving past touch slop cancels both, and the list scrolls instead.
- **`FR-STATUS-3.2` The checkbox is its own target.** It consumes its own down and up and **claims** each press (`CheckboxPresses`), so Also today's reorder never starts from the checkbox; holding anywhere else on the row still reorders.
- **`FR-STATUS-3.3` Row long-press is unchanged:** All Tasks opens the status sheet (now redundant, harmless); nothing elsewhere.

`FR-STATUS-4` **One app-wide undo toast follows every status change made from a list.**

- **`FR-STATUS-4.1` Which changes:** a tap to Done or Open, and every single-task change made from the status sheet. **Whole-series changes** (freeze, close, thaw or reopen the series) show a toast **without Undo** — they delete occurrences, and the panel's button was already the confirmation. **Editor saves show no toast.**
- **`FR-STATUS-4.2` Undo restores exactly:** the task's status, completion time and to-do date (Backlog's cleared date included), and its habit logs and goal contributions **with the same ids and amounts**, never re-asking "How much?" (`TaskRepository.statusSnapshot` / `restoreStatus`; the ledger is touched only for moves into or out of Done). If the task has changed again since (e.g. through sync), Undo does nothing.
- **`FR-STATUS-4.3` One host** (`StatusChanges` in `AppContainer`, with its own app-lifetime scope; `StatusToastHost` outside the NavHost, above the bottom nav), so a toast survives tab switches and a change isn't cut off by leaving the tab. A new toast **replaces** the old one and takes its Undo. It shows for **6 s**, lengthened through `AccessibilityManager.calculateRecommendedTimeoutMillis` when TalkBack is on.
- **`FR-STATUS-4.4` Copy:** one line, the title ellipsised, then **Undo**. No "+1" (§7).
  - `Done · <title>`; when a Done ticked a habit or moved an amount-measuring goal, **the links replace the title**: `Done · ticked Write in the morning · moved First draft to 62.5K words` (compact amounts, one decimal at most; directional goals don't appear).
  - `Marked open · <title>`, `Moved to Backlog · <title>`, `Closed · <title>`, `Frozen · <title>`.
  - Whole series, no Undo: `Series frozen · <title>`, `Series closed · <title>`, `Series thawed · <title>`, `Series reopened · <title>`.

`FR-AUDIO-1.2` **(refined) The chime fires on Open → Done made from a list**, now in one place (`StatusChanges`) for all four lists. Undo is silent; editor saves are silent.

> **[OPEN] — Does a status-sheet pick of Done chime?** v2.4.0's `FR-AUDIO-1.2` excluded state-sheet transitions ("contemplative decisions, not the daily tick-it-off gesture"). As built in M7, `StatusChanges.change` chimes on every Open → Done that goes through it — taps **and** sheet picks. Nobody decided to drop the exclusion. To settle in verification: either confirm the new behaviour or pass a flag from the tap path only.

**Acceptance criteria**

## 7. `DM-NOT` audit

The deck was drawn without the locked anti-gamification decisions in view, and three of its details are replaced:

| Deck | Replaced with | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| A habit's subtitle `7-DAY STREAK`; "the streak pauses" | the habit's **static cadence** (`3× / WEEK`, `10 PAGES / DAY`, `DAILY`) — no weekly progress either (`FR-EDITOR-6.6`) | `DEC-2`, `DM-NOT` |
| The toast's `+1` | the plain verbs *ticked* and *moved* (`FR-STATUS-4.4`) | `DEC-2`, `DM-NOT` |
| A goal's `40%` | type-specific subtitles; never `Goal.pct()`'s placeholder percentage for a generic goal (`FR-EDITOR-6.7`) | `DM-NOT` (adherence %), honesty |

Also checked and clean: the changed dot means "changed" and nothing else; the status sheet's count badge is dropped (`FR-STATUS-1.5`) and the save sheet's badges count occurrences a choice reaches, not performance; Closed reads "never counted as a miss" and the sheet keeps "Nothing here is a failure."; ending, pausing or deleting a series records nothing against the user (`FR-REC-5.12`, `FR-REC-6.15`).
